Questions

Things people ask before they open an account.

What is a money market fund?

A money market fund is a collective investment scheme that pools money from many investors and invests it in short-term, low-risk instruments such as treasury bills, fixed deposits and commercial paper. Because the underlying holdings are short-term and high-quality, money market funds are designed to preserve capital while paying a competitive yield.

How is my interest calculated?

Interest accrues on your closing balance every day, based on the fund's current yield. Each morning, the previous day's interest is credited to your portfolio by increasing your unit count at the current net asset value. Because interest is credited as units, it compounds automatically.

How long does a redemption take?

Redemption requests are processed on each business day. M-PESA and bank transfers typically complete within 24 hours. Larger redemptions may take up to 48 hours depending on the settlement window of the underlying instruments.

Is there a minimum investment or lock-in period?

Minimum investment is KES 1,000. There is no lock-in period and no exit penalty. You can redeem any portion of your balance at any time.

How safe is my money?

RockWin is regulated by the Capital Markets Authority. Assets are held in trust by I&M Bank, separate from RockWin's own balance sheet. The fund is independently audited by Deloitte. Money market funds are not guaranteed, but they are designed to be among the lowest-risk collective investment schemes available in Kenya.

What documents do I need to open an account?

You need a valid Kenyan national ID or passport, your M-PESA phone number, an email address and the name, phone number and relationship of your next of kin. The entire process takes about three minutes online.
