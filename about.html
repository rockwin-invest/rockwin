Our Story

RockWin Investment was founded in 2024 with a simple belief: every Kenyan deserves access to professional-grade investment products, not just those with millions in the bank.

Traditional money market funds required paperwork, physical visits, and minimum investments that excluded most people. We built RockWin to change that — with a mobile-first platform that lets anyone start investing from as little as KES 1,000, in under 3 minutes.

Today, we manage over KES 2.4 billion in assets on behalf of 18,000+ investors across Kenya. Our fund is regulated by the Capital Markets Authority, held in trust by I&M Bank, and independently audited by Deloitte.

But we're just getting started. Our vision is to become Kenya's most trusted digital investment platform — offering a full suite of products that help people grow, protect, and pass on wealth.
