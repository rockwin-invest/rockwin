About RockWin

A savings and investment platform built for the way Kenyans actually save.

RockWin Investment exists to give Kenyans a serious, transparent home for their money — built mobile-first, with no paperwork and no lock-in.

Our story

Access to professional investment products in Kenya has historically required paperwork, physical visits, and minimum deposits that excluded most people. Simple, high-yield savings products were effectively out of reach for anyone outside a narrow slice of the market.

RockWin was built to close that gap. A mobile-first platform where anyone with a national ID, an M-PESA number, and KES 1,000 can open an account and start earning daily interest in about three minutes. No paperwork, no lock-in, no exit penalties.

The money you deposit stays in the pooled fund — it does not touch RockWin's operating accounts. You can see every deposit, every interest credit, and every redemption in your dashboard as it happens.

How we operate

Three principles, in order of priority.

  1. 01

    Your money is never ours

    Client deposits are held in a pooled fund structure, separate from RockWin's own balance sheet. That structural separation is the single most important safeguard on the platform.

  2. 02

    Every rate is published

    The current yield, management fee, and total expense ratio are published on the fund factsheet and updated monthly. There are no hidden charges, no exit fees, and no performance-based fees.

  3. 03

    Interest compounds daily, not monthly on a marketing slide

    Interest accrues on your closing balance every night at the current yield and is credited as units the following morning. Because it is credited as units, it compounds automatically — you do not need to do anything to earn the compounded return.
