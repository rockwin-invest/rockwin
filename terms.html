03 The investment

RockWin Investment operates an open-ended money market fund. When you deposit funds:

  • Your money is converted into units at the prevailing NAV per unit
  • Units represent your proportional ownership of the fund's underlying assets
  • Interest accrues daily and is credited as units each morning, so it compounds automatically
  • You may redeem your units at any time, subject to the redemption process

RockWin Investment does not guarantee returns. The fund's value may fluctuate based on market conditions.

04 Deposits & redemptions

4.1 Deposits

Deposits may be made via M-PESA, bank transfer, or other approved channels. Minimum initial investment is KES 1,000. Units are credited once payment is confirmed.

4.2 Redemptions

You may redeem units at any time. Redemption requests are processed within:

  • 24 hours for M-PESA payouts
  • 2–3 business days for bank transfers

A minimum balance of KES 1,000 must remain in your account unless you are closing it entirely.

05 Fees & taxes

RockWin Investment charges the following fees, deducted from fund assets (not from your account directly):

  • Management fee: 1.20% p.a.
  • Trustee fee: 0.15% p.a.
  • Custodian fee: 0.10% p.a.

Interest earned is subject to a 15% withholding tax as required by the Kenya Revenue Authority (KRA). This tax is deducted at source and remitted directly to KRA.
