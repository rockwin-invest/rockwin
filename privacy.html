Privacy Policy

RockWin Investment Ltd ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share, and protect your personal data when you use the RockWin Investment platform.

This policy complies with the Kenya Data Protection Act (2019) and the Capital Markets Authority regulations on client data handling.

1. Data We Collect

We collect the following categories of personal data:

Category Data Collected
Identity Data Full legal name, National ID / Passport number, date of birth, photograph (for KYC)
Contact Data Phone number, email address, physical address
Financial Data M-PESA number, bank account details, transaction history, portfolio value, units held
Next of Kin Name, phone number, relationship (CMA requirement)
Tax Data KRA PIN, tax consent status
Technical Data IP address, device type, browser, login timestamps

2. How We Use Your Data

We process your personal data for the following purposes:

  • KYC verification — to comply with CMA and anti-money laundering (AML) laws
  • Account management — to operate your investment account, process deposits, and execute redemptions
  • Interest accrual — to calculate and credit daily interest to your portfolio
  • Tax compliance — to deduct and remit 15% withholding tax to KRA
  • Communications — to send SMS alerts, email statements, and regulatory notices
  • Fraud prevention — to detect and prevent fraudulent activity
  • Service improvement — to analyse usage patterns and improve the platform

3. Legal Basis for Processing

We process your data based on:

  • Contractual necessity — to deliver the investment services you signed up for
  • Legal obligation — to comply with CMA, KRA, and AML regulations
  • Consent — for marketing communications and optional features
  • Legitimate interest — for fraud prevention and service security

4. Data Sharing

We may share your data with:

  • I&M Bank — as Trustee and Custodian
  • Deloitte — as Independent Auditor
  • Capital Markets Authority — for regulatory reporting
  • Kenya Revenue Authority — for tax remittance
  • Africa's Talking — for SMS delivery
  • KYC verification partners — for identity verification

We do not sell your data to third parties.

5. Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active, plus 7 years after account closure, as required by CMA regulations. After this period, data is anonymised or securely deleted.

6. Your Rights

Under the Data Protection Act (2019), you have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Request deletion (subject to regulatory retention requirements)
  • Object to certain processing
  • Request data portability
  • Withdraw consent (for optional processing)

To exercise these rights, contact: privacy@rockwin.co.ke

7. Data Security

We implement the following security measures:

  • 256-bit encryption for data in transit and at rest
  • Two-factor authentication for account access
  • Role-based access control for internal staff
  • Regular security audits by third-party firms
  • Daily backups stored in encrypted form

8. Cookies

Our platform uses cookies and similar technologies to maintain your session and remember your preferences. You can disable cookies in your browser, but some features may not function correctly.

9. Children

Our services are not intended for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated via SMS and email at least 14 days before they take effect.
