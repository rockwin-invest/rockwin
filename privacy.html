Privacy Policy

How RockWin Investment collects, uses, and protects your personal data.

Last updated 11 October 2026

Contents

RockWin Investment Ltd ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, how we use it, and what rights you have over it.

This policy complies with the Kenya Data Protection Act (2019) and applicable data handling regulations.

01 Data we collect

We collect the following categories of personal data when you open or use a RockWin account:

Category Data collected
Identity Full legal name, National ID or Passport number, date of birth, and any supporting documents required for identity verification.
Contact Phone number, email address, and postal or physical address.
Financial M-PESA number, bank account details, transaction history, portfolio value, and units held.
Next of kin Name, phone number, and relationship.
Tax KRA PIN and tax consent status.
Technical IP address, device type, browser, and login timestamps.

02 How we use your data

We process your personal data for the following purposes, each tied to operating your account or complying with the law:

  • Identity verification — to comply with anti-money laundering laws
  • Account management — to operate your account, process deposits, and execute redemptions
  • Interest accrual — to calculate and credit daily interest to your portfolio
  • Tax compliance — to process statutory deductions where required by law
  • Communications — to send statements, alerts, and notices
  • Fraud prevention — to detect and prevent fraudulent activity
  • Service improvement — to analyse usage patterns and improve the platform

03 Legal basis for processing

Under the Data Protection Act (2019), we rely on the following legal bases:

  • Contractual necessity — to deliver the investment services you signed up for
  • Legal obligation — to comply with applicable financial and tax regulations
  • Consent — for marketing communications and optional features
  • Legitimate interest — for fraud prevention and account security

04 Data sharing

We share personal data only with organisations that are necessary to operate the platform or meet our legal obligations. These may include:

  • Trustee and custodian partners — to hold and administer pooled assets
  • Independent auditors — to verify financial records
  • Regulatory and tax authorities — for statutory reporting
  • Payment and messaging providers — to process deposits and deliver notifications
  • Identity verification partners — for identity verification

We do not sell your data to third parties.

05 Data retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active, plus 7 years after account closure, as required by applicable regulations. After this period, data is anonymised or securely deleted.

06 Your rights

Under the Data Protection Act (2019), you have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Request deletion, subject to statutory retention requirements
  • Object to certain processing
  • Request data portability
  • Withdraw consent for optional processing

To exercise any of these rights, contact privacy@rockwin.co.ke. We respond to verified requests within 30 days.

07 Data security

We apply the following safeguards to protect your data:

  • Encryption in transit — all traffic uses TLS
  • Encryption at rest — sensitive data is encrypted in the database
  • Role-based access control — internal staff only see the data their role requires
  • Regular security reviews — periodic reviews of access and dependencies
  • Encrypted backups — daily backups stored in encrypted form

08 Cookies

Our platform uses cookies and similar technologies to maintain your session and remember your preferences. You can disable cookies in your browser, but some features may not function correctly.

09 Children

Our services are not intended for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

10 Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated by email at least 14 days before they take effect.
