Fund Overview

RockWin Money Market Fund is an open-ended unit trust scheme regulated by the Capital Markets Authority (CMA) of Kenya. The fund invests in short-term, high-quality money market instruments including treasury bills, fixed deposits, and commercial paper issued by reputable institutions.

The fund's objective is to provide investors with a secure, liquid investment that delivers competitive returns while preserving capital. Units are priced daily and interest accrues every day, giving investors full transparency on their earnings.
