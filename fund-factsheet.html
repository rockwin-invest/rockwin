Fund overview

What the fund is, and what it invests in.

RockWin Investment operates an open-ended pooled fund. The fund invests in short-term, high-quality instruments — treasury bills, fixed deposits, and commercial paper issued by reputable institutions.

The objective is to provide investors with a secure, liquid home for their savings that delivers competitive returns while preserving capital. Units are priced daily and interest accrues every day, so investors have full transparency on their earnings.

Portfolio composition

The fund maintains a diversified portfolio with a weighted average maturity under 365 days, ensuring high liquidity and stability while maximising returns.

Treasury bills (91-day & 182-day) ~52%
Fixed deposits (commercial banks) ~28%
Commercial paper (A-rated institutions) ~12%
Call deposits & cash ~8%
Weighted average maturity ~180 days
