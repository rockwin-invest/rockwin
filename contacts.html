Common questions

These cover most enquiries. If yours isn't here, message us on WhatsApp.

My deposit hasn't shown up yet. What should I do?

Deposits via M-PESA usually confirm within a few seconds. If it's been more than 10 minutes, send us a WhatsApp message with the M-PESA confirmation code and we'll trace it. If the STK prompt never appeared, the transaction didn't start — try again from your dashboard.

How do I redeem funds?

Open your dashboard → tap Redeem → choose M-PESA or bank account → enter the amount. Requests are reviewed same-day, and funds typically reach you within 24 hours for M-PESA, or 2–3 business days for bank transfers.

How is interest calculated?

Interest accrues on your closing balance every day at the fund's annual yield divided by 365. The amount is credited to your portfolio as additional units each morning, and because those units earn interest too, the return compounds automatically. Interest is subject to 15% withholding tax, deducted at source.

How do I update my profile or change my password?

Sign in and open Settings from the sidebar. You can update your name, email, phone, and password there. Your national ID and date of birth cannot be changed online for compliance reasons — contact us if either needs correcting.

Can I download a statement?

Yes. Open your dashboard and click "Download statement" under Resources. You'll get a PDF with your account details and full transaction history, generated on demand.

Is RockWin regulated?

For details on regulation, trusteeship, and our audited financials, see the fund factsheet or contact us directly.
